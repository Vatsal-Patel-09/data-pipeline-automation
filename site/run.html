    What to try

    Switch the load strategy and watch the clock change. Row-at-a-time sends one INSERT per row, which is 85,400 sequential network round trips at roughly three tenths of a second each. The replay compresses it, because the real thing takes over seven hours.

    Something the measurement exposed

    Of the 35.03 s write step, only 17.39 s is loading. The rest is five separate connection handshakes, one per table, because the loader opens its own connection when none is passed in. Reusing one would roughly halve that step. Listed as open work rather than quietly fixed in the prose.
